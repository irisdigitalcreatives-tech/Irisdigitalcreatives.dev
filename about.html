FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before we work together.

Here’s what clients usually want to know.

What can you automate?+

I can automate lead capture, follow-up, CRM updates, notifications, customer communications, task routing, onboarding, marketing workflows, data synchronization, reporting processes, and many repetitive operational tasks. The exact architecture depends on how your business currently operates.

Do I need to replace my current software?+

Not necessarily. I first assess the tools you already use. When possible, I connect and improve your existing stack through native integrations, APIs, webhooks, Zapier, Make, n8n, or GoHighLevel instead of forcing unnecessary platform changes.

Can you work with GoHighLevel?+

Yes. My GoHighLevel work includes CRM configuration, pipelines, workflows, lead capture, forms, funnels, calendars, contact management, automated email/SMS follow-up, and integrations with external systems.

Can you build AI automations?+

Yes. I work with AI-powered workflows, AI agents, LLM-based systems, automated customer interactions, routing logic, and integrations connecting AI with business applications.

Can you improve an existing automation?+

Yes. I can audit the current workflow, identify unnecessary steps, broken logic, duplicate actions, poor handoffs, missing error handling, and opportunities to simplify the architecture.

How do you start a project?+

I start by understanding the business objective and current workflow. Then I map the process, identify automation opportunities, design the architecture, build the system, test edge cases, and refine it before deployment.

How does automation save a business money?+

Automation reduces the time employees spend on repetitive administrative work, minimizes avoidable manual errors, improves response speed, and allows teams to focus on work that requires human judgment, selling, service, and strategy.

Can you work with a business that doesn't know what to automate?+

Yes. You don't need to arrive with a technical specification. Show me how the business currently works, where your team spends time, and where problems occur. I can help identify processes that are strong candidates for automation.
